## Stage 11 boundary

Stage 11 defines the universal Emulator API/Core and independent CPU, Memory, Video, Audio, Input, Timing and Storage abstractions plus adapter discovery. It intentionally contains no console-specific emulator and no UI.

## Remaining stages

Stages 12–31 remain part of the established 31-stage project law and are not renumbered or replaced by this correction. Their exact original labels must be copied from the fixed plan before Stage 12 starts; no new names are to be invented.

## Global architecture law

- unlimited independent cores;
- self-contained modules;
- no direct module-to-module coupling;
- Web, Android, Telegram and future targets isolated through adapters;
- performance and emulation capability before decorative graphics;
- minimal UI with no decorative drawings or emoji;
- no portal-wide economy;
- no stage skipping;
- no claim of executed tests when the environment has not actually run them.

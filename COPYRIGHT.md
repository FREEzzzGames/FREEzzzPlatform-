## Third-party game ROMs

The four Mega Drive ROMs tested with the emulator are not covered by the
project's MIT License:

| ROM | Rights status |
|---|---|
| Mortal Kombat 3 (Europe).md | Commercial copyrighted game; no redistribution license verified |
| Mortal Kombat II (World).md | Commercial copyrighted game; no redistribution license verified |
| WWF WrestleMania - The Arcade Game (USA, Europe).md | Commercial copyrighted game; no redistribution license verified |
| Simpsons, The - Bart vs. the Space Mutants (USA, Europe) (Rev A).md | Commercial copyrighted game; no redistribution license verified |

The research confirms that WWF WrestleMania: The Arcade Game was commercially
published by Acclaim/Midway, and that The Simpsons: Bart vs. the Space Mutants
contains copyright and licensing notices for Acclaim and The Simpsons rights
holders. These facts do not establish permission to redistribute ROM files.

For Mortal Kombat II and Mortal Kombat 3, no reliable source located in this
review established an open license permitting redistribution of the ROM
images.

## Important

A copyright notice is not a distribution license. The presence of a ROM dump,
a game title, an emulator, or a license for the surrounding source code does
not itself create a right to publish the ROM.

Only a separate license, permission, or other legally sufficient basis from
the relevant rights holder can authorize public redistribution.
